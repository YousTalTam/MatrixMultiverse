MULTIVERSE SYNERGIES

HOW TO PLAY

1

Set Up & Pick a Universe

Enter a callsign, choose a Combat Protocol (game mode), then select DC or Marvel to load its 90-hero roster.

2

Fill All 9 Category Slots

The board has 9 categories: Strength, Speed, Durability, Intellect, Combat, Ability, Stamina, Agility, and Black Market Value. Every category needs exactly one hero bound to it.

3

Pick a Hero, Then Bind It

Tap a hero card from the pool, then tap "Bind Hero" on the category slot you want to fill with it. Each hero can only be used once per draft.

4

Score = Stat x Weight

Filling a slot scores that hero's stat in that category multiplied by their overall weight (rarity). A high-weight hero in their best category almost always beats a low-weight hero anywhere.

5

Chase Team Synergies

Drafting a themed group, like the Bat-Family or the X-Men, unlocks a percentage bonus applied to your entire final score. Check the Synergies panel to see which sets are active in your universe.

6

Finish For Your Tier

Once all 9 slots are filled, your total is graded out of 100 (plus any synergy bonus) and ranked from Street-Level Vigilante all the way up to Multiversal Anomaly.

Game Modes

  • STANDARD: 10 heroes in the pool at a time, no time limit. Plan carefully.
  • QUANTUM BLITZ: A random category is forced on you with only 3 choices and a 7-second timer. Fast instincts only.
  • BLIND LORE: Names and art are hidden. You draft using only each hero's lore description.
  • SABOTAGE PROTOCOL: Ban 8 heroes before drafting starts, then steal 2 finished picks from your opponent at the end.

Pro tip: don't just chase the highest raw stat. A mid-tier hero with high weight in their specialty often out-scores a top-tier hero used outside their strength, and a completed synergy set can be worth more than several category wins combined.
